# aPFA v1.3.1

So.. we have many many Black MIDI players for Android, but not one seemingly has tried to replicate PFAs engine...
**UNTIL NOW!** 
Over the few months or so, me and HexagonMIDIs have been working on something called "aPFA" or "Android PFA" that intends to be a direct replication of PFAs engine to Android! It's in a state to where I'm confident enough in saying that you can legit run Black MIDIs on your phone in good faith!

aPFAs current features as of **v1.3.1** are:
- Manual/Automatic core affinity chooser. (can choose various cores, or if you don't care, aPFA will choose your strongest core!) 
- Track skimming (if a little slow, aka you can go to random points in the MIDI) 
- A 1:1 of PianoFromAboves actual engine! 
- Background color chooser along with custom images
- Color profiles!
- Legacy OpenGL ES 2.0 renderer
- **Pagefiling! (and chunked paging) for very large MIDIs!** (with SD cards too for 64-bit!)
